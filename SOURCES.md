## Where sources disagree

- **Affected range.** PHP's advisories and the CVE.org records list only 8.2 to 8.5 (below the fixed releases). NVD's CPE has no lower bound, so it marks everything before 8.2.35 as vulnerable. Google Cloud says "5.4 through 8.5.11", Calif says the earliest affected path dates to 5.4.0, and PHP itself shipped source backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1 and says 8.1 and older get no updates. Our reading: treat every PHP before the fixed releases as affected, including end-of-life branches.
- **SAPIs.** php.net names PHP-FPM, mod_php, php-cgi, FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed. The GHSA texts discuss mod_php (confirmed), FPM (susceptible; 109619 session path validated, 109618 FPM exploit not demonstrated), FrankenPHP and LSAPI, but do not discuss php-cgi. No source names the CLI SAPI. Red Hat's rating assumes FPM, not mod_php.
- **Severity.** PHP, NVD and Ubuntu: critical, CVSS 4.0 9.2. Red Hat: Important.
- **The upload-progress setting.** PHP and JFrog: partial; blocks the confirmed session paths only. VirusTotal: "a stopgap, not a fix" and community analysis "suggests it may not cover every vector". None call it a fix.
- **Release date.** php.net announcements and release JSON: 21 Oct. php.net ChangeLog: 22 Oct. Infosecurity says the leaked post claimed 22 Oct. Google's blog is dated 22 Oct.
